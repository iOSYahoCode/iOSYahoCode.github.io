Sky Courier

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Sky Courier (the “Game”) handles information. The Game is developed and published by Yaroslav Homziak, an individual developer based in Ukraine (“I”, “me”). If you have any questions, contact me at yaroslav.homziak@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The Game has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous gameplay and device statistics, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, to help improve the Game.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. I never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (completed levels, best times, in-game fuel and purchases status) locally on your device. This data is not sent to me. Deleting the Game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. To serve, personalise and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewarded ads completed).

Google’s use of this data is governed by Google’s own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the Game’s settings (“Privacy settings”). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • iOS: the Game asks for permission to track through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your IDFA is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads: the optional “No Ads” purchase turns off non-rewarded ads.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The Game uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive reports when the Game crashes or encounters an error, so problems can be found and fixed. A crash report may include the app instance identifier, device and operating system information, app version, the state of the Game at the time of the crash and a technical stack trace. Crash reports do not include your name or contact details and are kept for up to 90 days.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms and privacy policies. I receive only the information needed to deliver the purchase (for example, that a product was bought and a purchase receipt). I do not receive your name, email or payment card details.

Game balance server

When online, the Game may contact my server to download game balance parameters (for example, the in-game fuel price). No personal data is sent. Like any web server, it receives your IP address as part of the connection; server logs are used only for security and troubleshooting and are deleted within 30 days.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played (for example, which levels are hard, where players stop playing, which features are used) and to improve it. Firebase may collect:

  • an app instance identifier generated for your installation of the Game;
  • your device’s advertising identifier, where permitted by your consent and device settings;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version, approximate country/region);
  • gameplay events (for example, level started, level completed, ad watched, purchase made) and session information.

Analytics data does not include your name or contact details. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, analytics and advertising storage follow the choice you make in the consent form. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. See Privacy and Security in Firebase for how Google processes this data.

Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or under U.S. state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. I do not store personal data about you on my own systems; most requests concern data processed by Google for advertising and analytics — you can manage it through the choices above and through Google My Ad Center. For any request, contact me at the email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device. Server logs are kept for no longer than 30 days. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. Other data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with the effective date at the top.
